# europe

Country directories in lowercase ASCII kebab-case.

Source: https://github.com/mledoze/countries (country names and geographical regions).
Coverage: UN members, independent countries and Palestine, Kosovo and Taiwan.
Territories and dependencies are excluded. Kosovo and Taiwan are included as practical geographic entries without implying political recognition.
The `australia` repository represents the Oceania region.

Country directories: 46

## Major cities

Source: GeoNames `cities15000.zip`, https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ .
Minimum population: 15,000. Names are lowercase ASCII kebab-case. Duplicate names within a country receive GeoNames numeric identifiers.
City directories: 8159

## Provisional local areas (2026-10-17)

Added 2561 GeoNames PPLX (section of populated place) directory candidates within 1 km of a matching major-city point and matching admin1/admin2 codes. These are **proximity-based candidate associations**, not independently verified official municipal boundaries. Do not use for legal, electoral, administrative, or address verification. Provenance, GeoNames IDs and distances: `LOCAL_AREAS_PROVISIONAL.jsonl`. Original city directories are recoverable from branch `backup/pre-local-areas-20261017`. Unmatched features are intentionally omitted. Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ .
